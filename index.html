01 · House Rules

Six simple rules

Enough structure to keep things fair, without turning deckbuilding into a list of things you can't do.

01

£75 deck budget

Your complete 60-card deck, including Oathbreaker and Signature Spell, must cost no more than £75. Basic lands are excluded from the budget and count as £0.00; nonbasic lands count normally.

02

£7.50 card limit

No individual nonbasic-land card may have a price higher than £7.50. Basic lands count as £0.00.

03

Lowest card price

Use the lowest available regular-printing price. Postage doesn't count. Agree on a shared price source before submitting decks.

04

Oathbreaker legality

Normal Oathbreaker deckbuilding rules and the official ban list apply.

05

No command-zone infinites

No infinite combos involving your Oathbreaker and/or Signature Spell. Other combos are allowed.

06

One mass removal spell

Maximum one mass removal spell in your main deck (creatures, artifacts, lands, etc.). It cannot be your Signature Spell. Graveyard recursion is allowed.

02 · Spirit of the game

Play hard. Keep it friendly.

These are guidelines, not extra bans. Creative decks and powerful cards are part of the fun.

✧

Command-zone synergy

Avoid overwhelming, difficult-to-interact-with combinations that are guaranteed from the start.

∞

Combos are fine

Strong interactions are welcome. Try not to build around consistently ending games before they've really started.

↗

Interaction matters

Removal, counterspells and combat tricks are encouraged. Everyone should have chances to respond.

♜

Casual power level

Build something that can win, without feeling obliged to optimise every game into the same result.

05 · Quick clarifications

A few common questions

Can I play Windfall, tutors or other powerful cards?

Yes. These cards are allowed if they're legal in Oathbreaker and meet the price limits. Strong cards are not banned just for being strong.

Can my Signature Spell be an instant or a sorcery?

Yes. It must be an instant or sorcery that fits your Oathbreaker's colour identity. Under these house rules it cannot be a mass removal spell.

Does Armageddon count as mass removal?

Yes. Mass removal includes sweeping away creatures, artifacts, enchantments, lands or other large groups of permanents, not just creature board wipes.

Can I return my board wipe from the graveyard?

Yes. You can have one mass removal spell in the main deck and reuse it through normal graveyard recursion.

Are combos in the main deck allowed?

Yes. The explicit combo ban is for infinite combos involving the command-zone cards. The wider guideline is to avoid reliably ending games before other players get involved.

Build to win. Let everyone play.

Make something memorable, enjoy the big turns and leave room for your friends to fight back.
